# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers screening for applied AI/ML roles (about 70% of applications: LLM engineering, RAG, geospatial/remote-sensing AI, applied ML) and data roles (about 30%: SQL, data science, analyst). They arrive from a resume, LinkedIn or a job application, usually on a phone or laptop, with a few seconds to decide whether to shortlist. Success in the first 30 seconds is that they contact Rochan or open a project.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Rochan Shrish Awasthi, B.Tech Computer Engineering student (SVPCET, Nagpur, Aug 2023 to May 2027) focused on speech processing, LLMs, computer vision and geospatial AI. It exists to be linked from a resume and applications, and to make a credible, substantive case from real evidence. The owner edits all content himself through a built-in admin (Projects, Experience, Skills, Achievements, site settings, custom fields) with no developer involved.

## Positioning

Evidence first: every project is told as problem, approach, measured result, backed by real benchmarks, competition placements, live deployments and a published paper. The page makes no claim it cannot show.

## Operating Context

Next.js on Vercel (free), Supabase for data, auth and image storage. Content is seeded from profile.md and edited afterwards in /admin. The site must stay free to run indefinitely and fully self-editable. It is read-only to visitors; one owner account.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: Hero (name, role line, bio, photo), snapshot stats, Experience, Achievements, Projects (with status, optional image, links, custom fields), Skills, contact links.
- Custom fields per content type render publicly with no code change.
- Content is dynamic and may be short or long; the design must hold with 0 to many projects and with or without images.
- Responsive to small phones; visible keyboard focus; sufficient contrast; prefers-reduced-motion respected; semantic HTML.
- Do not fabricate metrics, testimonials or URLs. Several items in profile.md section 14 are unresolved; the owner decides wording.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Rochan Shrish Awasthi. A real hero photo exists and is used.
- Binding look constraint: keep it light and restrained, professional and credible. Not dark, not neon, not a generic SaaS template. Colours are not pinned and may change.
- Voice: plain, factual, first-person-free; no hype.

## Evidence on Hand

Benchmarks (e.g. Dice 0.538 vs 0.149 baseline, 86.7 mAP, 85% Whisper accuracy and 81.83% UAR), 1st of 43 finalists at TruliaCare India, Top 30 of 8,744 at ISRO BAH 2025, ISRO Geospatial AI Challenge winner, published paper (IJIRCCE 2026), two internships, 13 GitHub repos, live deployments. Real project screenshots mostly do not exist yet; the owner will supply some. No testimonials exist and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with proof: the strongest measured result is the first thing a skimmer remembers.
2. A visitor can reach contact and the best work within seconds, on a phone.
3. Honest about stage: prototypes and unresolved claims are labelled as such.
4. Everything shown is editable by the owner without code.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard operability, respect reduced motion, usable at 200% zoom and on small phones.
