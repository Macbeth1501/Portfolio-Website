# Home page surface brief

Scope: public home page (/). Mode: Experience (portfolio read by recruiters). Audience: recruiters screening AI/ML (70%) and data (30%) roles; success = they contact Rochan or open a project within 30 seconds, on phone or laptop. Content: dynamic from Supabase; achievements and project results are the proof. Constraints: light, restrained, credible; free hosting; owner edits all content; no fabricated claims; project images arrive later (authored placeholders until then). Memorable moment: the graticule and contour lines draw in once on load.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one survey sheet of Rochan's work. It refuses the minimal dev-portfolio arrangement (big name, hairline list, chip cloud) and instead reads the way a topographic sheet does: title block, legend, sheet index, spot heights.
OWN-WORLD: Cool pale sheet (not cream), survey-ink blue-black text, hydrography blue for links, vegetation green for measured results, road amber for in-progress, faint contour brown for linework. Neatline double border with graticule ticks and a coordinate colophon. Archivo display, Public Sans body, JetBrains Mono for numbers and coordinates. Benchmark triangle markers, contour plates, legend box, sheet-index grid. 2px corners, no shadows, no cards.
STORY: A recruiter understands in seconds who this is, sees the legend (stats and contact), the sheet index to jump to anything, and meets proof as large spot heights rather than small print; they contact or open a project.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: name as the sheet title in large Archivo, role line, bio. Right column: hero photo with corner registration marks, below it the 2x2 sheet index. Behind the name, contour lines draw in. Directly under: a bordered Legend box holding the snapshot stats and the contact links as one row.
FORM: Survey Sheet, candidate 3 of the grounded list (research preprint was my pick), seed key e7d4f936, degraded roll with no challengers.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
